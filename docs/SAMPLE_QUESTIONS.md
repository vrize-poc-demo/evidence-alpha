# Sample Questions For Testing

Use these questions during demo or validation.

## 3M 2022 10-K

Filing:

```text
3M_2022_10K
```

Question:

```text
Is 3M a capital-intensive business based on FY2022 data?
```

Expected evidence pages:

```text
47, 49, 51
```

## 3M 2018 10-K

Filing:

```text
3M_2018_10K
```

Question:

```text
What is the FY2018 capital expenditure amount (in USD millions) for 3M? Give a response to the question by relying on the details shown in the cash flow statement.
```

Expected answer:

```text
$1577.00
```

Expected evidence page:

```text
59
```

## 3M 2022 10-K Operating Margin

Filing:

```text
3M_2022_10K
```

Question:

```text
What drove operating margin change as of FY2022 for 3M? If operating margin is not a useful metric for a company like this, then please state that and explain why.
```

Expected theme:

```text
Operating margin decreased, driven by gross margin pressure and special items such as litigation and PFAS-related charges.
```

## Not Found Check

Use a question that is not supported by the selected filing:

```text
What was 3M's revenue from its Mars operations in FY2022?
```

Expected behavior:

```text
Not found in this filing.
```
